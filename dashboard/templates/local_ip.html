Device listener

This listener serves device traffic only. MQTT, Internet access, OTA and private chat are not required. Messages have challenge/HMAC integrity protection; ordinary HTTP does not encrypt measurements. Credentials are provisioned locally and never shown here.

Loopback is the safe initial setting. Choose an address assigned to the intended adapter. Do not use 0.0.0.0, a public address or port forwarding.



Permitted devices
Enroll devices through the existing device registry first. A direct Node additionally needs its own locally provisioned token. Permission does not create credentials or imply physical connectivity.
